# Control plan

| What | Measure | Limit or target | How | Frequency | Owner | Reaction if out of limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lead time per new hire | Working days, offer accepted to ready | Individuals chart limits from the pilot (UCL 18.2 days) | Report from HR system and IT tickets | Weekly | HR operations lead | Case review within 2 days, find the step that waited |
| Ready on day one | Share of new hires ready on their start date | ≥ 80%, p-chart limits per month | Same report | Monthly | Head of HR operations | Root cause review at the monthly operations meeting |
| IT order at offer acceptance | Share of equipment orders created within 1 day of acceptance | ≥ 95% | Automatic order triggered by the HR system | Weekly | IT service desk lead | Check the trigger and the order queue |
| Access request quality | Share of access requests sent back | ≤ 10% | IT ticket status | Monthly | IT service desk lead | Update the standard request form, brief the senders |
| Hiring manager timing | Share of requests less than 10 days before start | ≤ 15% | HR system | Monthly | HR business partners | Reminder to the managers concerned, raise in leadership meeting |
| Equipment stock at site C | Days of stock of standard laptops | ≥ 10 days | Asset system | Weekly | Site coordinator | Reorder, move stock from site A |

## What changed (Improve)

| Root cause | Change | Type |
|---|---|---|
| IT equipment ordered only after the contract was signed | The equipment order is created automatically when the offer is accepted, so delivery runs in parallel with the contract | Process redesign (remove a hand-off) |
| One in four access requests sent back for missing information | A standard request form with required fields and role-based access profiles | Mistake-proofing (poka-yoke) |
| Hiring managers asking late | Automatic reminder at offer acceptance, and late requests visible in the monthly report | Standard work, visual management |
| Longer equipment delivery at site C | Minimum stock of standard laptops at site C | Buffer stock |

## Handover to the process owner

* The process model, the control plan and the report are handed to the head of HR operations.
* The control charts run for 12 weeks after the pilot. With no rule violations, the project closes and the measures move into the monthly operations report.
* Savings are confirmed with finance using the control-phase data, not the pilot.
